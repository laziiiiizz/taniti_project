Discover Taniti's Natural Wonders

Explore all that our beautiful island has to offer. From mountain peaks to rolling valleys, there's something for everyone.

Popular Activities

  • Boat and bus tours of the island
  • Hikes in the lush rainforest
  • Visits to Taniti's active volcano
  • Chartered fishing tours
  • Snorkeling in crystal-clear waters
  • Zip-lining adventures through the rainforest canopy
  • Scenic helicopter rides

Whether you're seeking adventure or relaxation, Taniti's diverse landscape offers unforgettable experiences for every type of traveler.
